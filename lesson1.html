What is Prayer?

The Definition

In Islam, Salah (Prayer) is a ritual act of worship that serves as a direct link between a servant and their Creator. It is not merely physical movements but a spiritual dialogue.

Why We Pray

We pray to find peace, to express gratitude, and to seek guidance. It is a moment of stillness in a busy world, allowing our souls to breathe and realign with sacred truth.
